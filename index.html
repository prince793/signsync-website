The Gap

Classrooms move fast. Communication shouldn't be what slows a student down.

In SPED classrooms, sign language is often the clearest way a student can express what they know but not every teacher, aide, or classmate can read it in real time.

01

Limited FSL fluency

Few teachers and support staff are fluent in Filipino Sign Language, creating a daily translation gap in instruction and assessment.

02

No real-time bridge

Existing tools are either too slow, too generic, or not built for a classroom's pace leaving gestures unread until it's too late to respond.

03

Hardware that isn't classroom-ready

Sign recognition research rarely accounts for shared devices, variable lighting, or the physical setup of an actual SPED room.

How It Works

From gesture to understanding, in four steps.

SignSync pairs an IoT capture unit with an AI recognition pipeline built specifically around Filipino Sign Language.

01

Capture

The IoT unit captures hand movement and gesture data directly in the classroom.

→
02

Track

Hand landmarks are isolated in real time, filtering out noise from the room around them.

→
03

Recognize

The AI model matches tracked movement against trained Filipino Sign Language gestures.

→
04

Translate

The result is surfaced instantly readable by the teacher the moment it's signed.

Features

Built around a real classroom, not a lab demo.

Real-time recognition

Gestures are processed and translated with minimal delay, keeping pace with classroom conversation.

FSL-specific training

The recognition model is trained specifically on Filipino Sign Language, not adapted from foreign sign systems.

IoT-integrated capture

A dedicated capture unit handles gesture input, keeping the system independent of any single device.

SPED-classroom ready

Designed with input from an actual SPED setting, accounting for shared use, lighting, and pacing.

Continuous feedback loop

Every session helps refine recognition accuracy for the gestures used most in that classroom.

Built-in assistant

An in-app chatbot supports learners and teachers with guidance while they use the system.
